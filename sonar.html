Sonar echo ranging

Send a short audible chirp and look for its reflection. This experimental tool suggests possible echoes, not an exact wall distance.

Ping a wall

In a quiet room or corridor, stand roughly 1 to 3 metres from a flat, hard wall. Point the bottom edge of your phone towards it, keeping the speaker and microphone clear. Disconnect headphones or Bluetooth speakers, set the speaker to a moderate volume and keep the phone away from ears.

The page processes sound locally. Nothing is uploaded.

Find the reflection

Repeat the ping from a second spot farther from the wall. The wall echo should move to a longer delay. Peaks that don’t move may be other reflections or interference. The strongest peak need not be the wall echo.

    Distance ≈ speed of sound × extra travel time ÷ 2. We use 343 m/s and set zero at the presumed direct chirp, not at the instant play was tapped. Speaker and microphone spacing and room echoes can affect the estimate.
