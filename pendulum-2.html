Pendulum period

Point the phone camera at a swinging pendulum. The page tracks the bob and times each full swing.

  1. Make the bob a bright colour (a coloured ball, or a sticker on it). Use a plain background.
  2. Prop the phone steady, camera facing the pendulum. The pendulum must swing across the picture, not towards the camera.
  3. Tap Start camera, then tap the bob on the picture so the page learns its colour.
  4. Pull the bob back about 15° or less and let go.

The video is processed on your phone and is not uploaded or saved. The page finds the bob in each frame, works out the line it swings along, and times the gap between one swing and the next (T). Expected T = 2π√(L/g), with g = 9.8 m/s². This holds for small swings only. A large swing makes T a bit longer. Camera rate is usually 30 fps, so each timing has a resolution of about 0.03 s, but averaging many swings gives a much better value.
