Colorimeter

Find the concentration of an unknown coloured solution. Photograph your standards, build a calibration line from absorbance, and read the unknown off it.

1 · Prepare your solutions

Make a stock (for example, one drop of food colouring in 100 cm³ of water), then five standards by diluting it: 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% and 100% stock. Keep one unknown sample from your teacher or a helper. Use identical cups, the same water level, the same background and the same lighting for every photo.

2 · Get a reading

Start the camera, or load a photo, then tap the middle of the liquid in the picture. Or type the R, G, B values by hand.

3 · Standards

For each standard, get a reading (step 2), type its concentration, then press “use” on that row. Concentration can be in any units you like — % stock or mol dm⁻³ — as long as you use the same units for the unknown.
