Hang the phone from a string, inside a sock or small bag tied tight. Hang it low over a bed or carpet in case the knot slips. Measure the string from the pivot to the middle of the phone and type it in below. Tap Start, pull the phone back a small angle (about 15° or less) and let go so it swings in a straight line.

#T (s)
The page uses the phone's gyroscope. It works out which way the phone rotates as it swings, then times the gap between one swing and the next (T), so the phone can hang in any orientation. Expected T = 2π√(L/g), with g = 9.8 m/s². This holds for small swings only. A large swing makes T a bit longer.
