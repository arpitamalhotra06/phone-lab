Pendulum period

Point the camera at a swinging pendulum. Tap the bob once — the page tracks it, measures the period T, and works out the length of the string.

1 · Set it up

Hang something brightly coloured (an orange ball, a keyring toy) on a string from a doorway or shelf, in front of a plain wall. Prop the phone up so it stays still, with the bob swinging left–right across the view. Keep the swing small — under about 15° either side — for the best result.

2 · Track the bob

Start the camera, then tap the bob to lock onto its colour

The page finds the period from upward crossings of the midpoint of the bob’s motion, then uses T² = 4π²L/g. The camera needs HTTPS (GitHub Pages is fine). No camera? The demo data shows the whole analysis. Large swings make T longer than the small-angle formula expects, so the page’s length comes out a little too long — keep the swings small.
