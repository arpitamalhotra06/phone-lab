Elevator ride

Use your phone’s accelerometer to see how apparent weight changes when a lift starts and stops.

Record a ride

While the lift is stopped, hold the phone flat, screen up, and tap Start recording. Keep still until the page says to start the ride. Don’t walk, tilt the phone or lock the screen during the recording. Tap Stop after the lift has stopped.

Waiting to start.

Positive acceleration means you feel heavier; negative means lighter. Near zero can mean either constant speed or standing still. The graph shows acceleration, not velocity.

Optional: estimate height from pressure

iPhone Safari does not supply barometer readings to this page. If you already have pressure readings taken in the same lift while stopped at each floor, enter them here. You don’t need pressure readings for the acceleration experiment.
