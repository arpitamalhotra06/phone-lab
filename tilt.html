Tilt angle

The page works out how far the phone is tilted from flat, using the direction of gravity. Check it against a protractor.

  1. Tap Start. Lay the phone flat on a table and tap Zero. This removes the sensor's small offset.
  2. Rest the phone on a book, a board or a ramp at an angle. Keep it still.
  3. Measure the angle of the board with a protractor, type it in, and tap Record.

Tilt from flat = arccos(|z| / |a|), where z is the reading along the screen's normal and |a| is the total acceleration. Gravity is the only thing the sensor sees while the phone is still, so the tilt is the angle between the screen and the ground. The reading is the average of the last half second. This does not depend on which way up the phone is or on iPhone and Android sign differences. It measures the angle of the surface the screen lies on, so lay the phone flat on the slope.
