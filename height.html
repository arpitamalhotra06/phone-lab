Drop height meter

Try the demo first. For a live trial, tap Start and hold the phone still until it says Ready. Release it from about 5–10 cm above the centre of a thick pillow on a bed. Do not push or spin it, and never drop it onto a hard surface.

Height = ½ g t², with g = 9.8 m/s² and t the time the phone spent in free fall. Threshold = 0.5 × the phone's own reading at rest, measured when you tap Start. Free fall is detected when |a| (gravity included) stays below the threshold for at least the minimum duration (80 ms by default). Very short falls can be missed. After each drop the next 1 s is ignored so bounces are not counted. Sampling rate and sensor filtering vary by phone. The height is an estimate, and a soft landing can shift the detected end. Demo trials use simulated sensor data.
