Ruler

Point the camera at something with a known size (a credit card is 8.56 cm wide, a coin, a metre stick). Tap its two ends, type the real length to set the scale, then tap any two points to measure the distance between them.

Swing timer

For any repeated motion: tap the button each time the object passes the same point going the same way (for a pendulum, once per full swing). The page averages the intervals and gives the period.

Tone generator

A pure sine tone for resonance tubes, sonometers and the Doppler experiment. Keep it between 300 and 5000 Hz for the best microphone response.

Pitch meter

Listen to any steady sound — a tuning fork, a plucked string, your tone generator — and the page shows the dominant frequency and the nearest musical note.
