Settings are worked out for you. Min duration = 0.6 × √(2h/9.81), so it scales with the drop height. Threshold = 0.5 × the phone's own reading at rest, measured when you tap Start. Free fall is detected when |a| (gravity included) stays below the threshold for at least the min duration. g = 2h / t². After each drop the next 1 s is ignored so bounces are not counted. Sample rate is about 50 to 60 Hz, so t has a resolution of roughly 0.02 s.
