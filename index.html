#t (s)min |a|g (m/s²)
Free fall is detected when |a| (gravity included) drops below the threshold for at least the minimum duration. g = 2h / t². Measure h from where the phone leaves your hand to where it lands. After each drop the page ignores the next 1 s so bounces are not counted. Sample rate is about 60 Hz, so t has a resolution of roughly 0.017 s.
