Phone Lab

Pick a subject, then an experiment.

Biology

1Specimen lab

For teacher-supervised labs. Pick an animal, study the organs, explore a photo of a specimen, and take a quiz.
2Animal behavior

Put pillbugs or fruit flies between two conditions, like moist and dry. The page times the trial, counts each side with tap counters, and tests whether the preference is statistically real.

Physics

1Drop height

Drop the phone onto carpet or a bed. The page times the fall and works out how far it dropped.
2Pendulum period

Point the phone camera at a swinging pendulum. The page tracks the bob and measures the time of one full swing, T.
3Tilt angle

Rest the phone on a slope or a book. The page measures the angle from gravity, and you check it with a protractor.
4Ramp and friction

Let the phone slide down a slope. The page measures the acceleration and works out the friction.
