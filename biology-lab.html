Specimen Lab

A companion for teacher-supervised labs. It helps you study a real specimen; it never tells you where or how to cut.

Before you start

Use this only during a lab approved and supervised by your instructor. Follow your institution's specimen-handling rules and ask about non-animal alternatives if you prefer them. Specimens should be ethically and legally sourced.

Privacy: the camera feed and photos stay in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded. Only your notes and quiz progress are saved on this device.

Modules

    What is real, manual, simulated, or missing?

    Functional Camera viewing, freeze/upload, notes, pins, quiz, PNG and text export.

    Manual You place two landmarks (snout tip and vent). The reference overlay is positioned from them, and you correct it by dragging.

    Simulated The overlay is a schematic reference of typical frog anatomy, not a measurement of your specimen. Hidden structures are predictions and individuals vary.

    Not implemented Automatic recognition, live overlay tracking while the camera moves, other species, microscopy, and any server processing. A static page cannot do these.
