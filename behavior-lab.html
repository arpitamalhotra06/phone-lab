Question you are testing

Do the organisms show a preference between two environmental conditions (for example moist vs dry, light vs dark)? You set the two sides, record where animals are at timed intervals, then test the result against a 50/50 expectation.

Use only organisms and conditions approved by your instructor (common classroom examples: pillbugs/isopods or fruit flies). Handle gently, keep conditions non-harmful, and return organisms as directed. This page records observations — it does not tell you how to set up or modify the chamber.

1. Experiment setup

Randomizing which physical side is Condition A is a control for side bias — it stops a real preference from being confused with "animals just like the left side."

3. Statistics — is the preference real?

We pool the final counts of all trials and compare them with a 50/50 expectation using a chi-square goodness-of-fit test (1 degree of freedom). If the statistic exceeds the critical value of 3.84, the result would be statistically significant at the 0.05 level — evidence of a preference rather than random movement.

Lab notes

Phone Lab — Animal Behavior module. This page is a timed observation recorder and statistical analyzer. It does not identify organisms, detect motion automatically, or guide chamber construction. Repeated counts of the same animals measure preference within a trial; they are not independent samples. Educational use only — not a scientific publication tool.
